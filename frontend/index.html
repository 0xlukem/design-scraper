What can an analysis establish?

A bounded view of a public page. It does not establish every page, responsive size or interaction state. Check the evidence before reusing an inferred value.

What limits apply?

Daily, per-visitor and capture limits apply to included use and your own key. The workspace checks availability when you begin. Saved designs remain available without an analysis.

How is my key handled?

Your key stays in this tab’s memory and is sent over HTTPS through our server to your chosen provider. It is never saved. Your provider’s charges apply only when you use your own key.
