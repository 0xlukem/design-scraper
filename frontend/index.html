Frequently asked
questions.

What is Design Scraper?+

Design Scraper examines a public webpage using screenshots, CSS evidence and AI vision. It turns what it finds into a structured design reference you can inspect and export for your next build.

What can I extract from a website?+

Explore colors, typography, spacing and component styles from the captured page. The result keeps measured values, AI interpretations and missing evidence distinct.

How accurate is a website design analysis?+

A capture covers a bounded view of a page, rather than every screen size, page and interaction. Review each value’s evidence, confidence and known gaps before using it.

Which formats can I export?+

Copy or download Extended Markdown, Compact Markdown, CSS variables, Tailwind-compatible CSS and design tokens JSON. All five come from the same result, without another AI request.

How does Mix It combine two websites?+

Mix It uses two website references to propose a new design system. Choose their relative influence with the mix balance control, then inspect the resulting values, sources and gaps.

Do I need an API key to start?+

You can analyze through Design Scraper’s shared Gemini service while capacity is available. You can also explore and export saved designs without starting an analysis.

Can I use my own AI provider?+

Yes. Choose Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or Grok in the workspace and use your own API key. Your provider bills your account; displayed costs are estimates, and shared capture and admission limits still apply.

How is my API key handled?+

Your key stays in tab memory and travels over HTTPS to the server and your chosen provider for analysis. The application does not persist the key. Provider processing and retention follow the provider’s terms.

What are saved designs?+

Saved designs are dated analyses of third-party websites, rather than official brand guides or current captures. Opening them and copying or downloading their exports uses static assets, without a new analysis request.

Which websites can I analyze, and what limits apply?+

Design Scraper analyzes accessible public HTTP(S) pages. Daily, per-visitor, concurrency and capture limits apply with both shared access and your own key. Check availability in the workspace; saved designs remain available when new analyses cannot run.
