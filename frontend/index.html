Know what you’re
working with.

What does a capture establish?

Static visual branding from a bounded page region. It does not establish every page, a complete responsive system or unvisited interaction states. Video and audio playback are excluded. AI roles and recipes can be interpretations; review their evidence and known gaps.

What limits apply to a new analysis?

Shared daily admission, per-IP, concurrency, capture and AI budgets determine availability. Check the current allowance in the workspace before starting. Your own key funds AI usage, but does not remove shared capture and admission limits.

What will it cost?

With your own key, your provider charges your account under its terms. The workspace shows configured models and estimated token rates. Missing pricing is unavailable, not free. Displayed costs are estimates, not invoices.

Can I use a result without running an analysis?

Yes. Open any saved design, inspect its evidence, copy values or download an export. That needs no key and makes no new analysis request. Each result reflects its original analysis date.
